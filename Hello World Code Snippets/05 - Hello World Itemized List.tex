Hello, world! \\

Why is \LaTeX\ so popular with scientists? One big reason is that it's good with math and equations. Consider, for example, the equation for a linear regression: $$Y = \alpha + \beta_1x_1 + \beta_2x_2 + \ldots + \beta_nx_n + \epsilon$$

Can you imagine any easier way of typesetting such an equation? \\

\noindent
Other reasons why people like it:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{Free}
	\item{Open-source}
	\item{Cross-platform}
\end{itemize}
