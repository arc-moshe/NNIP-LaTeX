\textbf{Example of a Description List}

\end{center}

\begin{description}

\item[Bilbo Baggins]

A hobbit who goes on an unexpected adventure there and back again.

\item[Gandalf the Grey]

A wizard who recommends Bilbo Baggins to the party of dwarves to serve as the thief on their adventure.

\item[Thorin Oakenshield]

Son of Thrain, son of Thror, and the direct descendent of Durin, he is king in Exile of leader of the company of dwarves who seek to reclaim the Lonely Mountain from Smaug. 

\end{description}
